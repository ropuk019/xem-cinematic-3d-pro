# 3D typography

## Hierarchy
One headline should dominate. Keep supporting copy short. Exact spelling, punctuation, and brand casing are mandatory.

## Geometry
Extrusion, bevel, and rounded edges create dimensionality. Avoid excessively deep extrusion that harms letter recognition. For inflatable typography, use a rounded display font and soft specular response; true inflated geometry may require a 3D modeling step.

## Mobile legibility
Test at actual output size. Avoid thin strokes, low contrast, and text placed over busy reflections. Use a local licensed font to ensure reproducibility.

## Logo integrity
Use supplied logo assets as-is unless the user explicitly requests a redesign. Never invent or silently alter a brand mark.
