# Lighting

## Three-point foundation
- Key: primary form-defining source, usually broad and soft.
- Fill: lower-energy source controlling shadow density.
- Rim: separates silhouette from background.

## Product lighting
Use large area-like sources or broad directional/spot setups. Create long, controlled specular reflections on glossy objects. A reflection should describe shape, not create random bright noise.

## Environment
A dark background is not a substitute for lighting. Preserve enough fill to read material and silhouette. Use a floor plane and contact shadow for grounding. Avoid excessive ambient light that flattens the scene.

## Atmosphere
True volumetrics can be expensive and renderer-dependent. For lightweight motion graphics, layered transparent planes or subtle particles can suggest atmosphere. Label approximations honestly and keep them behind the subject.
